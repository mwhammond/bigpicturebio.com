01 / Redesign

Making sure resistance never emerges.

Standard of care shrinks a tumour, then resistant cells regrow and take back over. We look for combinations that hold every cancer population down at once, driving toward near-zero residual disease instead of a temporary response.

The opportunity

The right combination could raise the OS ceiling by up to 12x in the hardest cancers.

The same engine that predicted this year's ASCO read-outs to 87% projects that optimised combinations and sequencing could move some of the hardest cancers from a median overall survival of roughly 8–11 months to 84+ months, largely by re-combining assets already in clinical use. We are building those combinations in-house and with partners.
