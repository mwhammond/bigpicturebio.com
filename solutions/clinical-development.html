3.2 · Predicted tumour biology — Regeneron Ph III

Baseline receptor mapping placed 68% of tumour mass within an immune-excluded stroma. Reactive CAF sustain TGF-β–driven exclusion; absent stromal normalisation, PD-1 blockade cannot convert into durable CD8⁺ killing.

The regimen carried no stroma-directed arm. The model therefore predicted negligible CD8⁺ ingress and no curve separation beyond month 18, with AR-V7⁺ resistant subclones repopulating thereafter.

Predicted 24-mo OS Δ = +1.2 mo (n.s.) — matched the reported read-out.
