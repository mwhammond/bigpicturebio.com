Why TOPO payloads underperform in PDAC

Dense desmoplastic stroma limits payload penetration to <30% of the tumour core; TOPO-driven DNA damage is further buffered by high baseline replication stress tolerance in KRAS-mutant PDAC.

The model predicts a micro-tubule co-payload shifts the death mechanism to one the stroma cannot buffer — the orthogonal interaction, not the dose, is what moves the curve.

Implication: dual-payload ADCs, not higher TOPO potency, define the next SoC in this setting.
