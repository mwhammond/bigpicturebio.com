Our mission

Curing complex diseases requires combinations.

The largest therapeutic benefits come from combinations of drugs working together — and combinations are exactly where existing tools are weakest. The search space is intractable for conventional machine learning and impossible to brute-force in the lab, so the field has barely moved.

We build a reasoning world model that predicts the synergistic interactions deciding modern therapies: additive and synergistic effects, across cell populations, metastases and patient sub-groups, all the way to trial-site risk. It lets us identify the optimal combination and sequence of drugs to normalise the most aggressive diseases — and predict the outcome before a trial starts. We start in oncology, then move into the immune and neural systems where single-target thinking has failed for decades. The goal is simple: turn months into decades, for the patients who have run out of options.

A reasoning world model for combination therapy

We show that endpoint outcomes of novel drug combinations can be predicted prospectively and mechanistically — across diseases where no data on the specific agents exists — by reasoning over receptor engagement, resistance dynamics and trial design in one framework.
